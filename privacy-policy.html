Privacy Policy

Hadejia Data Hub — Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hadejia Data Hub ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website, mobile app, admin dashboard, or WhatsApp ordering service (together, the "Service") to purchase airtime, data, voice bundles, cable TV subscriptions, electricity tokens, or to fund your wallet.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: full name, email address, phone number, and a hashed transaction PIN.
  • Transaction information: purchase history, amounts, wallet balance, and wallet transaction history.
  • Identity verification (optional): if you request a permanent virtual bank account for wallet funding, your Bank Verification Number (BVN) is collected and sent directly to our banking partner (SecureWaveNG) for verification. We do not store your BVN in our own database.
  • WhatsApp messages: if you interact with our WhatsApp ordering bot, we process the messages you send us (such as the email/phone you use to verify your account, and your order selections) in order to fulfill your requests.
  • Technical information: IP address, device/browser information, and log data collected automatically for security and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and wallet.
  • To process and fulfill your purchases (airtime, data, voice, cable, electricity) through our service providers.
  • To process wallet funding through our payment partners.
  • To respond to support requests, including via WhatsApp.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to reconcile transactions with our providers.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share only the information necessary to fulfill your requests with:

  • AutosyncNG — to deliver airtime, data, voice bundles, cable TV, and electricity purchases.
  • SecureWaveNG — to process wallet funding by bank transfer, including BVN verification where applicable.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform — to operate our WhatsApp ordering service, if you choose to use it.
  • Supabase — our database and authentication infrastructure provider.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

Your transaction PIN is never stored in plain text — it is hashed before storage. Sensitive identifiers such as your BVN are transmitted directly to our verified banking partner and are not persisted in our own systems. Access to administrative functions is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and transaction information for as long as your account is active, and afterward for as long as necessary to comply with legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention obligations.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below. Note that we may need to retain certain transaction records to comply with legal obligations even after an account deletion request.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age required to hold a wallet/financial account under applicable Nigerian law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
